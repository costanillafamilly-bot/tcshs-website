01 — ABOUT TCSHS

MORE THAN A SCHOOL.

A place for curious minds.

Toledo City Science High School provides an environment where students are encouraged to explore science, technology, research, creativity, and leadership.

Through academic experiences and student-centered activities, TCSHS aims to develop learners who are prepared to contribute meaningfully to their community and to the world beyond the classroom.

02 — OUR STORY

THE TCSHS STORY

Rooted in excellence.

Toledo City Science High School is part of Toledo City's continuing commitment to developing students with strong foundations in science and technology.

It was founded in 1996 to provide quality science and mathematics education to students. It began with only 25 first-year students and was initially annexed to Bato National High School, which served as its lead school in the Toledo City Division. Since its establishment, the school has continued to focus on science, mathematics, research, and academic excellence. It aims to develop students' knowledge, skills, and talents to prepare them for future challenges.

06 — THE HOUSES

ONE SCIENCE FAMILY

Four houses. One TCSHS.

Every TCSHS student belongs to a house. Compete, collaborate, and earn points for your colors.

M
SNSKRIT • "REFLECTION"

Manthanein

Mathematics Inspired by the Sanskrit word manthana, meaning reflection, contemplation, or deep thought, Manthanaean symbolizes logical reasoning, intellectual curiosity, and the pursuit of understanding.

T
GREEK • "ART/CRAFT"

Techne

Technology Derived from the Greek word technē, meaning art, craft, or skill, Techne represents human ingenuity and the application of knowledge to create useful tools, techniques, and innovations..

S
LATIN • "TO KNOW"

Scire

Science Derived from the Latin word scire, meaning “to know,” Scire embodies curiosity, discovery, and the continuous pursuit of knowledge through observation and investigation..

H
ARABIC • "ENGINEERING"

Handasa

Engineering Inspired by the Arabic word for engineering and geometry, Handasa represents the art of designing, building, and creating practical solutions through innovation and precision.
